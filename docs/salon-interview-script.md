# Salon Interview Script

For hair salons, braiders, barbers, nail and beauty salons.
Takes about **15–20 minutes**. Visit at a quiet time, such as a weekday morning.

---

## Before you start: 5 rules

1. **Ask about the past, not the future.** "How many clients didn't arrive last week?" gets real answers. "Would you use an app?" gets polite ones.
2. **Don't pitch the app until the end.** You're here to learn about their problems.
3. **Write down numbers:** minutes, rands, clients per day.
4. **Ask "Tell me about the last time..."** and let them talk. Stay quiet after they answer; they often add the best details.
5. **Ask "Why?"** at least once in each section.

---

## 1. Introduction (say this)

> "Hi, I'm [name]. I'm building a simple booking tool for salons in [area], and I'm talking to salon owners to understand how bookings work today. It's not a sales visit. I just want to learn. Do you have 15 minutes? Can I take notes?"

---

## 2. About the salon

1. What kind of salon is this? (hair / braids / barber / nails / beauty / mixed)
2. How many people work here? How many chairs or stations?
3. Who handles bookings: you, a receptionist, or whoever is free?
4. Which days and hours are you open? Do you take lunch breaks or days off?

## 3. Services

5. What are your **5 most popular services**? For each one: **how long** does it take and **what does it cost**?

6. What is your **longest** service?
7. Do prices change? For example with hair length or the products used. How do you tell clients the price before they come?
8. Do clients often book **several services together**, like a wash + cut + blow-dry?

## 4. How bookings happen today

9. How do clients book? (WhatsApp / calls / walk-ins / Instagram / Facebook / other) Which is the most common?
10. How many clients do you see on a **normal day**? And on your **busiest day**?
11. How far ahead do clients usually book? (same day / 1–2 days / a week / a month)
12. Out of 10 clients, how many are **walk-ins**?
13. Where do you write bookings down? (paper book / phone calendar / WhatsApp chats / memory) **Can I see it?**
14. **Tell me about the last time a booking went wrong,** such as a double booking, a forgotten client or a mix-up.
15. How much time a day goes into answering "Are you free at 3?" messages or calls?

## 5. Staff (skip if they work alone)

16. Do clients ask for a **specific stylist**? How often?
17. Does each stylist have different hours or do different services?
18. How do you decide which stylist gets a walk-in?

## 6. No-shows and late cancellations

19. **Last week**, how many clients didn't arrive or cancelled late?
20. Which services were they for? Roughly how much money was lost?
21. What did you do after the no-show? Did you contact the client?
22. Do you take **deposits** today? If yes: how much, how do clients pay (EFT, card, cash), and do any clients refuse?
    If no: why not?
23. Do you remind clients before their booking? How?

## 7. Tools and money

24. Have you ever used a booking app (Fresha, Booksy, SweepSouth, Calendly, other)? What did you like or dislike? Why did you stop?
25. What do you **pay for today** to run the salon? (software, SMS, adverts, card machine)
26. How do clients pay? (cash / card / Yoco / SnapScan / EFT)
27. Does everyone at the salon have a smartphone with data during the day?

## 8. Wrap-up (now you can explain the idea)

> "The idea: clients book themselves from a link you share on WhatsApp. They get an automatic reminder, and you see your whole day on one screen, including walk-ins."

28. What do you like about it? What worries you?
29. What is the **one thing** it must do for you to use it every day?
30. If it cut your no-shows in half, what would a **fair monthly price** be? (Let them say a number first. Then ask: "What about R99?")
31. Would you try an **early free version** and tell me what's wrong?
32. Do you know **another salon owner** I should talk to?

> "Thank you, this really helps. Can I come back or WhatsApp you when I have something to show?"
